01 · About

Backend systems that stay fast as the business grows.

Senior Backend Engineer with 12+ years of experience designing, building, and maintaining scalable applications for SaaS platforms and data-driven systems. My core stack is Node.js, TypeScript, and PHP on AWS (Lambda, ECS, DynamoDB, S3), backed by PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Docker, and Kubernetes. I currently work at Lean-tech for Slalom, building highly available APIs and cloud-native backend services for enterprise workflow platforms. Before that, I spent six years at QRVEY, an embedded analytics SaaS platform, where I rose to lead a 4-person team building data ingestion pipelines and distributed services that helped the platform scale with the business. AI is part of my daily work: I use agents like Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and OpenCode, plus local models in LM Studio, and add custom skills and prompts to my projects to improve results. I'm now focused on bringing LLMs and agentic workflows into production backend systems, with the same emphasis on performance, reliability, and clean architecture (DDD, SOLID).
